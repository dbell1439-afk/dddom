# Recruiter Message + LinkedIn Connection Templates

Fill the `{{placeholders}}`. Keep them short, specific, and honest.

---

## 1. LinkedIn connection request (≤ 300 characters)
```
Hi {{first_name}} — I'm a bilingual biomedical professional (MSc Biopathology, WES-verified)
and Lead Phlebotomist at Tampa General, exploring {{role_type}} roles at {{company}}. I'd
love to connect and learn about your team. Thank you!
```

## 2. Short recruiter message (after applying)
```
Hi {{first_name}}, I recently applied for the {{job_title}} role ({{req_id_or_link}}). With
11 years of laboratory leadership, a WES-verified biomedical Master's, current U.S. hospital
lab experience, and Portuguese/Spanish fluency, I believe I'm a strong fit — especially for
specimen handling, lab operations, and bilingual patient care. Could we set up a brief chat?
Happy to share my resume directly. Thank you for your time.
```

## 3. Cold outreach to a hiring manager (no posting yet)
```
Hi {{first_name}}, I admire {{company}}'s work in {{area, e.g. oncology diagnostics}}. I'm a
biomedical scientist and hospital Lead Phlebotomist with cytology and lab-operations
background (WES-verified degrees) and Portuguese/Spanish fluency. If your team hires for
specimen processing, lab operations, or clinical research support, I'd welcome a short
conversation. Thank you!
```

## 4. Referral request (to a warm contact)
```
Hi {{first_name}}, hope you're well! I'm applying for a {{job_title}} role at {{company}}
({{link}}). Given my lab-leadership and phlebotomy background, I think it's a strong match.
Would you be comfortable referring me or pointing me to the right person? Happy to send my
resume. Really appreciate it.
```

> Honest positioning only: never state or imply a U.S. certification/license Fernando
> does not hold. Log the contact name/email in the tracker.
