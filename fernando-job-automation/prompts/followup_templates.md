# Follow-Up Message Templates

Concise, professional. Fill `{{placeholders}}`. The tracker sets follow-up dates
automatically when you run `jobbot.py applied "Title" "Company"`.

---

## A. 3 days after applying
```
Subject: Following up — {{job_title}} application

Hi {{first_name}}, I wanted to confirm my application for the {{job_title}} role
({{req_id_or_link}}) was received, and to reiterate my strong interest. My lab-leadership,
specimen-handling, and bilingual patient experience map closely to this role. I'd welcome the
chance to discuss it. Thank you!
— Fernando Rodrigues
```

## B. 7 days after applying (no response)
```
Subject: Still very interested — {{job_title}}

Hi {{first_name}}, following up on my application for {{job_title}}. I remain very interested
in contributing to {{company}}'s {{team/area}}. If helpful, I'm glad to provide references or
answer any questions about my background (WES-verified biomedical Master's, U.S. hospital lab
lead, Portuguese/Spanish fluency). Thank you for your consideration.
— Fernando Rodrigues
```

## C. After recruiter contact (keep momentum)
```
Hi {{first_name}}, thank you for reaching out about {{job_title}}. I'm excited about it. I'm
available {{2-3 time windows}} for a call. In the meantime, please let me know if you need
anything else from me. Looking forward to speaking.
— Fernando
```

## D. After an interview (thank-you, within 24h)
```
Subject: Thank you — {{job_title}} interview

Hi {{first_name}}, thank you for the conversation today about {{job_title}}. I enjoyed
learning about {{specific detail}}, and I'm even more enthusiastic about contributing my
{{specific strength discussed}}. Please let me know if I can provide anything further. Thank
you again for your time.
— Fernando Rodrigues
```

## E. After a rejection (keep the door open)
```
Subject: Thank you — and staying in touch

Hi {{first_name}}, thank you for letting me know. I appreciate the opportunity to be
considered for {{job_title}}. I remain very interested in {{company}}, and I'd be grateful to
be considered for future {{lab / research / phlebotomy}} openings that fit my background.
Wishing the team well.
— Fernando Rodrigues
```
