# Credential & Training Gap Analysis — Fernando Rodrigues

Purpose: when a strong job category repeatedly requires a credential Fernando lacks,
this table tells you whether it's worth pursuing. ROI = how much it increases realistic
access to $60k–$90k target roles relative to cost/time.

> The scoring engine already **auto-rejects** roles that hard-require MLS/MT/ASCP, a FL
> lab license, RN/MD/PA/PharmD with no pathway — so you don't waste time on them. This
> doc is about which credentials to *add* to unlock more of the queue.

## Recommended sequence (fastest income + access)
1. **This month:** Complete CITI GCP + Human Subjects and an ICH-GCP certificate. Add to the
   clinical-research resume. Keep BLS current.
2. **If phlebotomy-lead/donor roles dominate the queue:** add a national phlebotomy cert.
3. **If teaching roles appear:** pursue a phlebotomy-instructor credential.
4. **After landing a first CRC role:** pursue SoCRA/ACRP for advancement.
5. **Long term / optional:** investigate ASCP foreign-graduate eligibility — but do NOT let it
   block the near-term job search.
